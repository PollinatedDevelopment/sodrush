# 🌱 Sod Rush

A pixel-art endless runner where you play as a walking patch of sod, sprinting through themed worlds, dodging obstacles, taking down bosses, and racing other players online.

**Play it:** https://pollinateddevelopment.github.io/sodrush

## Features

- **13 themed worlds**, from Dr. Mulch's Farm to the Forbidden Dimension. Each has its own look, obstacles, and a boss at the end.
- **Neon Arcade, the bonus area.** Unlocked after the ending, it is a ten-district climb (levels 196-345) toward the Neon Tower.
  - **Ten districts:** Neon Arcade, Chrome Market, Holo Gardens, Circuit Docks, Glitch Quarter, Synth Canal, Laser Plaza, Vapor Heights, Data Slums, and the Neon Tower.
  - **Every district has its own scenery and themed obstacles**, and each one runs faster than the last.
  - **A cutscene opens the first level of each district**, with its own speaker and story. Tap, Space, or Enter advances it, and Esc or Skip ends it.
  - **District X is the final district.** The Neon King waits at the top (level 345) with 16 HP, three phases, and his own boss theme.
- Jump, double-jump, and slide through every run. Keyboard (Space / ↑ / W to jump, ↓ / S to slide) or touch (tap to jump, swipe down to slide).
- **Pause menu** with resume, restart run, and quit to lobby.
- **Outfits and hats.** Collect skins and hats from the shop and wear them in the dressing room. Every outfit has its own running animation, lobby animation, and sound effects.
- **Power-ups.** Spend seeds on Shield, Triple Jump, Head Start, or Seed Boost. Each purchase gives 3 uses, and you can equip one at a time.
- **Daily missions, a daily login streak, and achievements.**
- **Portal of Mystery.** A special challenge that comes around once a day.
- **Radio.** Pick any track from the game, including the Neon King's theme, and watch your character vibe to it.
- **Original soundtrack** with separate tracks for the lobby, runs, shop, boss fights, and more. District bosses use the Boss 1 track, and the Neon King has a track of his own.
- **Update log** in the lobby, so you can see what changed.
